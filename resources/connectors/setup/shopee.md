# shopee

## Entry
Shopee Open Platform; choose the app site from [[field:region]], not the sales market.

## Configure
Open App List and inspect the approved Seller in House System app. Check Shop, Product and Order access and the production keyset. Continue through the protected connection form to the official shop-account authorization.

## Credentials
Use [[field:shop_id]], [[field:partner_id]] and [[field:partner_key]] from that same production shop/app.

## Verify
Read the authorized shop identity and a bounded product or order list. A main/sub-account or Test Partner key is not the production shop grant.
