# lightspeed-x

## Entry
The merchant Retail POS X-Series account; locate personal-token settings through the official private-app/token guide.

## Configure
Inspect the intended account and prepare a dedicated Personal Token. Do not switch to another Lightspeed series or OAuth app just because its login page is easier to find.

## Credentials
Use [[field:store_domain]] and [[field:access_token]] as described by protected_fields.

## Verify
Read the store/outlet identity and a bounded product result; verify the domain and account if access fails.
