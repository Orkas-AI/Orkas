# Review data preparation

Record file/source, platform/store, product and variant, review ID, rating and scale, text, date and follow-up linkage. Keep unknown fields unknown. Exact source identifiers may be used internally for deduplication; deliver anonymized row references and relevant short excerpts without contact, address or order details.

Use a working evidence table: anonymous locator, source/store, variant, period, original excerpt and theme. At grouping time, retain each count together with representative locator–excerpt pairs, including repeated praise; emit the examples as ready-to-use `source / record or row — quotation` strings. For high-severity incidents, retain the complete locator and SKU index beside the group count, using compact record ranges or an appendix when long; one representative quote can accompany that index but does not replace its incidents. A shared text value can describe hundreds of rows, so selecting its representative source is part of aggregation rather than later copywriting. Preserve these citation strings when translating or composing the report. If only aggregate counts were supplied, cite that aggregate and retain its granularity instead of inventing review-level records.

Deduplicate repeated export records by platform + store + source review ID, retaining follow-ups as linked events rather than new independent customers. Where IDs are absent, flag likely duplicates for review; identical short praise alone does not prove duplicate authorship. Record raw rows, excluded/duplicate records, unique reviews, reviews with text and reviews with ratings separately. Conflicting duplicates need a disclosed resolution; do not silently overwrite.

Preserve SKU and period groups before comparing. An unknown variant belongs in its own bucket. Translate multilingual text for analysis while retaining original evidence; do not merge different complaints merely because translated wording is similar. Split support tickets from public reviews and do not combine their denominators without an explicit corpus definition.

Taobao/Tmall, JD and Pinduoduo follow-ups may share an initial review; Amazon parent/child variation linkage may change; Shopify review exports can be app-specific; Shopee/Lazada/TikTok Shop use market-specific exports. Resolve the supplied schema, not a guessed universal field name. For any other platform apply the same provenance and deduplication rules.
