# VOC output

Lead with the main supported finding and prioritized action.

- Corpus: source, window, raw rows, deduplication, included unique reviews, ratings/text availability, variant mix and selection bias.
- Themes: positive drivers and pain points with unique-review counts/denominators, variant/time scope and severity. Carry each selected excerpt together with its anonymous source locator from the working evidence table, including positive examples and severe incidents. A source appendix is useful for a large corpus; counts alone do not locate an example.
- Root causes: reported symptom, candidate explanation, supporting/conflicting evidence and the observation that would distinguish alternatives. Confidence refers separately to the reported pattern and to the explanation; repeated reports can establish a pattern while its physical cause remains untested.
- Actions: product, page, service or operations change, reason, priority and verification metric.

For comparisons show each group's own denominator and coverage. Do not imply overall market prevalence from an uploaded sample or give a chapter-length report when the requested decision needs a short answer.
