# Root causes and actions

Separate the reported symptom, possible cause, corroborating or conflicting evidence, confidence and validation needed. A complaint about leakage does not prove a defective batch without batch/test evidence; an expectation gap does not automatically mean misuse.

Map supported findings to product/design, supplier/quality, packaging/fulfillment, page/creative, or support actions. Service promises and packaging improvements are proposed changes until actually implemented; never turn them into current listing claims.

Prioritize by severity, supported frequency, affected variant/cohort, confidence, actionability and cost. A low-frequency severe incident can outrank common cosmetic complaints. Do not manufacture a numerical score from arbitrary weights.

Return action, owner capability, evidence, expected mechanism and a measurable follow-up. Full page writing and final product-selection decisions are downstream tasks, not substitutes for this analysis.
