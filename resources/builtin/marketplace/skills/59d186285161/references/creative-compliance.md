# Creative claim checks

Check both visible wording and implied visual outcomes against product evidence. A disclaimer does not prove an exaggerated demonstration. Keep packaging, certifications, accessories, test results, prices and promotion conditions accurate for the displayed variant.

Record asset provenance and permitted use in the production brief. Use market/category/placement-specific official rules only when relevant, with source and verification date; if unavailable, name the unverified constraint. Do not export one country's absolute-word rule to every platform or language.

For a main-image slot, secondary image, content video or paid ad, establish the actual format and overlay requirements. Keep finished copy separate from evidence placeholders. This review establishes brief readiness, not rendered quality, rights clearance for unknown assets, platform approval or publication.
