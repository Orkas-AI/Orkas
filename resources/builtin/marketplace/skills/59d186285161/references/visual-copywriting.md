# Visual copywriting

Build a message card: audience and scenario, buying objection, supported benefit, factual proof, source asset and intended placement. Prioritize one message per image and make the product/variant recognizable.

Distinguish product-main-image slots, secondary infographics, brand hero images and promotional ads. Their overlay and layout requirements differ; use the actual placement brief or verified current rules. Amazon, Taobao/Tmall, JD, Pinduoduo, Shopify, Shopee and other storefronts do not share one mandatory image sequence or copy allowance.

A useful optional sequence is product identity → benefit → specification → use → objection/offer. Choose the number of images to match the task. Keep text readable at delivery size; Chinese character-count rules do not transfer to another language or aspect ratio. Put dimensions, included quantity and compatibility where they prevent a wrong purchase.

For localized revisions preserve the approved message and unchanged product facts. Do not turn a price edit into a new design direction.
