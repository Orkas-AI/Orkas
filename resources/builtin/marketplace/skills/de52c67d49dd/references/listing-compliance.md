# Claim and channel checks

For each material claim establish source, variant, test conditions, market and validity period. Ratings, sales, certifications, performance, comparative results, stock urgency, discounts and service promises all require relevant evidence. Customer opinions can be attributed as opinions; they do not certify technical performance.

Do not turn an unsupported claim into a supported one merely by weakening its wording or adding a disclaimer. If a precise outcome lacks proof, use an evidenced factual feature or omit it from finished copy; list the required evidence separately. A literal specification such as 100% cotton is not the same as an unproven 100% success claim.

Check the target market/category/channel's current official requirements when a statement or field depends on them. Record the source and verification date for decisive constraints. Do not apply a global banned-word list, invent legal advice, or transfer one platform's rules to another. When access is unavailable, state the unverified requirement while completing the supported draft.

Verify promotions against actual dates, eligibility, price basis and included items. Avoid importing a competitor's assets or proprietary claims into the merchant's factual brief. Publication and platform approval are separate outcomes from copy drafting.
