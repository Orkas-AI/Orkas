# Amazon listing and A+ drafting

Resolve marketplace, product type, variant and the requested module. Use current seller requirements or [Product Type Definitions](https://developer-docs.amazon.com/sp-api/docs/product-type-definitions-api) when exact fields are needed; access to documentation does not establish seller eligibility.

Organize title around product identity and decisive attributes. Give bullets distinct buyer questions: purpose, supported benefit, specification/compatibility, use and included items or evidenced service terms. The requested field contract determines bullet count and length; do not assume one universal title limit.

A+ can explain materials, use steps, product family comparisons and brand context when eligible. Avoid repeating every bullet. A proposed comparison must match actual specifications, and a review quote cannot become an independently proven performance claim.

Separate a main product image brief from secondary infographics and promotional images. Verify the target slot's restrictions before adding copy or overlays. Image order, module count and layouts are proposals, not a guarantee of platform approval.
