# Amazon evidence adaptation

Scope marketplace, category, keyword set, parent/child ASIN, seller offer and observation date. Treat BSR as a category/time-specific rank, review counts as accumulated feedback and third-party sales as estimates. Neither ranking nor low review count establishes profit or easy entry.

Compare realized price and pack size, fulfillment mode, recent entrants, brand concentration within the stated sample, variation-level complaints and seasonal availability. Request current fee quotations using the actual dimensions, weight, category and fulfillment plan; do not embed fixed FBA or referral rates.

Keyword and reverse-ASIN reports can reveal competing search exposure. They do not establish incremental demand or free organic traffic. Any opportunity index needs its provider definition and remains supplementary.

For exact source fields or current requirements consult the relevant [Selling Partner API documentation](https://developer-docs.amazon.com/sp-api/docs) or the merchant's current seller report. No Amazon data service is installed by this Skill.
