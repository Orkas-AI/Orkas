# Evidence and channel context

Maintain a compact claim ledger: one narrow conclusion or value, source file/row or direct URL, platform, store, market, product and variant, period/timezone, currency, source field, definition, collection time and evidence status. Assign observed / calculated / estimated / hypothesis / unavailable per claim; one response may mix these statuses. A visible third-party estimate remains an estimate even when copied exactly.

Bind each supplied number to its stated meaning and basis before using it: for example total startup budget, per-item selling price, landed unit cost, acquisition spend or a proposed test ceiling. When an unlabeled amount has multiple decision-changing meanings, keep the amount, answer conditionally or name the provisional interpretation, and ask at most one focused follow-up after delivering the supported discovery work.

Decompose a bundled intake field into only the claims its value actually expresses; leave the other components unavailable. A category or sourcing direction alone does not establish supplier access, pricing, customization, capacity, fulfillment or another existing operating capability.

Before delivery, reconcile every external, current, numeric or comparative factual claim with evidence actually read in this task. Map each retained source-backed claim to the direct source record that supports that claim and its scope. One observed platform or macro claim does not upgrade unrelated category demand, competition, conversion, repeat purchase, supplier or cost claims. A report name, direct link or fact recalled without an observed source record is not evidence. When no relevant source has been supplied or opened, make the recommendation from stable category reasoning as explicitly unverified hypotheses and omit report names, links, rankings and current statistics. Remove an unsupported claim or label it as an unverified hypothesis and state the smallest check needed. Keep proposed trial allocations, thresholds and target prices labeled as plans; do not present them as supplier quotes, market norms, realized costs or merchant-approved spend.

For cross-platform comparison map fields explicitly before combining them. Listings, parent products, SKUs, orders, units, buyers, ratings and review texts are different grains. Match pack quantity, variant and price basis; discounts, taxes, shipping, currency and time windows can invalidate comparisons. Keep an unknown variant separate. Deduplicate overlapping exports without joining unrelated platform IDs.

## Coverage levels

1. Materials: work from supplied tables, documents and accessible pages for any named channel, provided fields and definitions can be established.
2. Adaptation: map that channel's fields and verify only relevant current requirements from its official documentation or supplied seller records. Record market, category and verification date. Missing documentation means adaptation remains provisional.
3. Connection: an available authenticated tool must actually support the requested data or action. A URL, reference entry or open-source project is not an installed adapter.

## Channel-specific questions

| Channel | Resolve before interpreting the evidence |
| --- | --- |
| Taobao / Tmall | Item versus SKU; displayed sales window, coupon and membership price; retail versus promotion period |
| JD | Merchant/fulfillment model; item and variant matching; price and service promise |
| Pinduoduo | Pack size; group, subsidy and other price conditions; shipped versus refunded orders |
| Douyin / Kuaishou | Product versus content or live-session metrics; paid and creator exposure; orders versus settled revenue |
| Amazon | Marketplace, parent/child ASIN and seller offer; ranking/category/date; fulfillment and fee quotation |
| Shopify / WooCommerce | Store-specific variants and reports; sales versus payment/return events; installed theme/app fields |
| TikTok Shop | Shop market, listing/variant, creator and paid attribution, settlement and reversals |
| Shopee / Lazada | Country store, item/model or variant, campaign vouchers, shipping subsidy and currency |
| AliExpress | Destination, shipping/tax basis, pack and variant, cross-border lead time |
| eBay | Active versus sold/completed offer, auction versus fixed price, condition and shipping |
| Etsy | Listing/variation, customization, production method, made-to-order lead time |
| Walmart | Seller item/variant, fulfillment model, offer price and observed marketplace scope |
| 1688 | Supplier and wholesale offer, quantity tier, MOQ, specification and landed cost; not retail demand |

These are analysis questions, not a claim of live API or current policy coverage. For an unlisted platform use the same ledger and explicit field mapping, not the nearest platform's guessed rules.
