# Chinese retail and sourcing evidence

Use the channel questions in [evidence context](data-provenance-and-confidence.md). Search-led, content-led and price-led are hypotheses about the requested channel and category, not universal platform identities.

For retail comparison record item/SKU, pack, displayed price conditions, sales field and window, shop, fulfillment, reviews, collection time and source. Separate creator/live activity from item demand. Inspect promotion and return effects before calling a spike an opportunity.

For 1688 sourcing record offer and supplier identifiers, tiered quotation, MOQ, specification, lead time, tooling, inspection and freight. Compare at the actual order quantity and intended retail quality. Retail competitors' sales and supplier transaction counts are not interchangeable. A sample, inspection or verified qualification can reduce a specific uncertainty; a platform badge alone cannot.

Use uploaded exports or supported authorized sources. A missing login or unavailable data source leaves a stated evidence gap; it does not justify replacing records with fabricated estimates.
