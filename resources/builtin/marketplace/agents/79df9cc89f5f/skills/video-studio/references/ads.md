# Ads and e-commerce video

Read [video-craft](@skill/video-craft) and its [marketing guidance](@skill/video-craft/references/marketing.md)
for the idea, shape, claims and test versions; for a product sale, also its
[ecommerce guidance](@skill/video-craft/references/ecommerce.md) for the commercial brief,
script, source-aware shots and content review. Use its evidence-led choice of
structure rather than imposing a demonstration or offer on every product.

Keep product facts, source attribution and editable prices/specifications in
the existing files described in [timeline.md](timeline.md). That reference also
owns source framing and page geometry. Product appearance in generated footage
is covered by [product-fidelity.md](product-fidelity.md).

Versions reuse assets: see `video-studio`'s revision workflow and the marketing
guidance on test versions. Changing words or order does not require generating
the footage again.
