# Ecommerce videos

Read only for product listing or sales-creative work after route lock. This reference supplements the selected video route; one-shot deterministic EDIT still goes directly to stage-edit without direction or plan stops or the general creative workflow.

## Build the sales idea from evidence

Choose the commercial structure the product supports: demonstration, comparison,
explanation or promotion. Transfer a reference's hook/proof/CTA relationship to
the user's product rather than copying unrelated selling points. Anchor SKU,
appearance, price/currency, offer conditions and CTA in supplied facts; mark
missing facts, and never invent results, testimonials or discounts. Real product
assets carry product evidence; generated atmosphere does not establish efficacy.

Route by the needed control, not by “ecommerce”: cinematic product action may be
a direct model clip; exact editable offer copy may need local layers. Reuse the
actual product references. For reference-led local work use
[reference-recreation.md](reference-recreation.md). Keep approved copy and local
tracks separate so a later price/caption change reuses completed model footage.

## Product continuity

Map product shots and overlays to the selected SKU and evidenced product attributes. Use the existing source/reference and preserve/may-change boundaries. Keep source footage unchanged outside the requested edit; do not invent accessories, operating behavior or demonstrated results.

## Candidate inspection

Apply the review boundary in [production-method.md](../../video-router/references/production-method.md) first. Direct model outputs skip this inspection. For local work, inspect the actual candidate's source product placement, locally authored labels and factual overlays at representative times and after local revisions. Compare against the selected source, including across shot boundaries when present. Check factual overlays for readability without obscuring the product. Apply video-craft's text-contrast and caption-legibility criteria to these overlays even in a one-shot edit: use a solid contrasting text fill or a suitable backing on the actual footage, not a pale fill whose thin outline alone carries the letters. Judge rendered frames; OCR text alone cannot verify contrast. If the candidate cannot be inspected or a product attribute does not match, state the unverified or mismatched result in the handoff.
